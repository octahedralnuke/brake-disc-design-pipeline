    Interpretation

    Temperatures use a transient conduction model with prescribed, circumferentially averaged braking heat and assumed convection. Structural results envelope cold torque loading and the hottest thermal snapshot with full torque. Mounting-hole surfaces are idealized as rigidly fixed. Stress values are maxima at integration points, including the constrained mount region.

    Generic constant steel properties and a single stop support comparative educational screening. Fatigue, wear, pad contact, local moving hot spots, fluid flow, bolt flexibility and manufacturing qualification are outside the model.
